# Blind evaluation rubric

Score each response from 1 to 5 on every dimension. Compare the two anonymous systems per case before identifying an overall preference. A tie is valid.

- **Semantic fidelity:** It fulfills the prompt without invented facts, altered certainty, or missing required content.
- **Contextual fit:** Its order, detail, register, and emphasis suit the stated purpose and audience.
- **Naturalness:** Its language is idiomatic and plausible for a competent writer in that situation.
- **Clarity:** The reader can follow the content with appropriate cognitive effort.
- **Rhetorical restraint:** Structural and rhetorical devices perform identifiable work instead of displaying polish.
- **Constraint compliance:** It respects length, language, requested elements, and exclusions.

For each case, name concrete evidence for the preference. Do not reward a response merely for being shorter, more formal, more symmetrical, or more elaborate. Flag any change in meaning or certainty as a fidelity defect.
